ACME CORP – ACCOUNT ACCESS AND IDENTITY MANAGEMENT GUIDE
Version 2.5 | Last Updated: October 2024
Document Owner: Identity and Access Management (IAM) Team

==============================================================
SECTION 1: ACCOUNT SETUP AND ONBOARDING
==============================================================

1.1 New Employee Account Activation
Your corporate account is created 2 business days before your start date.
You will receive your credentials via secure SMS to your registered mobile number.
First login steps:
1. Go to https://login.acme.com and enter your temporary password.
2. You will be prompted to set a new password meeting policy requirements.
3. Register your MFA device immediately at https://aka.ms/mfasetup.
4. Install mandatory software from Software Center within 48 hours.
5. Complete security awareness training within your first week.

1.2 Corporate Email Naming Convention
Email format: firstname.lastname@acme.com
For duplicate names: firstname.middleinitial.lastname@acme.com
Alias: Employees may request up to 2 aliases via IAM Portal.
Display name changes: Submit request via HR → IT will update within 5 business days.

==============================================================
SECTION 2: PASSWORD MANAGEMENT
==============================================================

2.1 Self-Service Password Reset
To reset your password without calling helpdesk:
1. Go to https://passwordreset.acme.com.
2. Enter your corporate email and verify with MFA.
3. Choose reset method: answer security questions OR use MFA verification.
4. Set a new password meeting all complexity requirements.
5. After reset, update your password in Outlook, Teams, and other cached applications.

2.2 Password Synchronization Issues
If password change doesn't sync across all systems (SAP, VPN, Citrix):
1. Wait 15 minutes for Active Directory replication.
2. Lock and unlock your Windows session to force credential refresh.
3. For SAP: change SAP password separately via SAP Logon → Extras → Change Password.
4. For VPN: reconnect VPN client using new credentials after AD replication.

2.3 Account Lockout
Your account locks after 5 failed login attempts.
Automatic unlock: After 30 minutes of no failed attempts.
Immediate unlock: Call IT Helpdesk (identity verification via mobile OTP required).
Repeated lockouts: May indicate compromised credentials — contact IT Security.

==============================================================
SECTION 3: ACCESS REQUESTS AND PERMISSIONS
==============================================================

3.1 Requesting Access to Systems or Applications
All system access requests must follow the access provisioning process:
1. Submit request via GRC Portal (https://grc.acme.com) → Access Request.
2. Select the system, role, and access level required.
3. Provide business justification (minimum 50 words).
4. Approvals: Line Manager → System Owner → Security (for sensitive systems).
5. Standard SLA: 3 business days. Critical business requests: 24 hours with VP sponsorship.

3.2 Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
Acme uses RBAC across all major systems:
- Access is granted based on job role, not individual request by default.
- Role assignments are reviewed quarterly by system owners.
- When changing roles/departments, old access is revoked within 30 days.
- Emergency access (break-glass): available for critical operational needs via IAM Portal.

3.3 Shared Mailboxes and Distribution Lists
To request a shared mailbox:
1. IT Self-Service Portal → Email Services → Request Shared Mailbox.
2. Specify mailbox name, purpose, and list of members.
3. SLA: 2 business days.

To add/remove members from distribution list:
- Group owners can manage membership via Outlook → Address Book → Distribution List → Properties.
- For IT-managed groups: submit request via IAM Portal.

==============================================================
SECTION 4: REMOTE AND MOBILE ACCESS
==============================================================

4.1 Mobile Device Management (MDM)
All devices accessing corporate email or data must be enrolled in Microsoft Intune:
- Personal devices: Enroll via Company Portal app (BYOD policy).
- Corporate devices: Auto-enrolled at setup.
- BYOD devices: Only corporate data is managed; personal data is not accessible by IT.

4.2 Remote Desktop Access
Citrix Virtual Desktop: Available for employees requiring remote access to desktop.
Access: https://citrix.acme.com
Requirements:
- Must be on corporate VPN or have Citrix Gateway certificate.
- MFA required for every session.
- Sessions automatically terminate after 8 hours of inactivity.

4.3 Microsoft Authenticator App Setup
1. Download Microsoft Authenticator from App Store / Google Play.
2. Go to https://aka.ms/mfasetup and sign in with corporate credentials.
3. Click "Add method" → "Authenticator app" → scan QR code.
4. Test by signing out and signing back in.
5. For lost/new phone: call IT Helpdesk for emergency bypass and re-enrollment.

==============================================================
SECTION 5: OFFBOARDING AND ACCOUNT DEPROVISIONING
==============================================================

Upon resignation or termination:
- Account access is revoked on last day of employment.
- 30-day email forwarding may be configured (manager request required).
- Personal data must be removed by employee before last day.
- Corporate devices must be returned within 5 business days of exit.
- Former employees retain access to payslips via ex-employee self-service portal for 1 year.
